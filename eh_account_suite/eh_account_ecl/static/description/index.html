The purpose

Make the next finance decision clearer.

Expected Credit Loss measures the loss allowance on trade receivables under IFRS 9. Each run is scoped to one legal entity and carries a provision matrix of contiguous, non-overlapping ageing buckets; population reconstructs point-in-time receivable residuals at the reporting date and fails closed when a matrix band or installed exposure provider is incomplete. It computes the closing allowance, and posting recognises only the movement from the opening allowance in one balanced entry: an increase debits impairment loss and credits a dedicated non-receivable loss-allowance contra account, while a decrease reverses it (IFRS 9.5.5.8). A general 3-stage model (EAD, LGD, 12-month and lifetime PD), an explicit keyed-versus-defaulted Stage 3 PD policy, probability-weighted scenarios and optional present-value discounting are supported. Foreign-currency write-offs preserve source-currency evidence. Posting and reversal are manager-gated; reversal uses an explicit open accounting date, and a computed or posted run is frozen until its controlled revision or reversal workflow.

Inside this module

Built around the work your team does.

Post only what changed

The run recognises the movement from the opening allowance to the closing allowance in one balanced entry, debiting impairment loss and crediting the loss-allowance contra account on an increase and reversing on a decrease (IFRS 9.5.5.8), never re-posting the full allowance.

Simplified or general

The simplified provision matrix measures lifetime ECL as gross carrying amount at each bucket's loss rate. The general approach measures EAD times LGD times PD, using 12-month PD for Stage 1, lifetime PD for Stage 2, and an explicit keyed-lifetime or defaulted-100% policy for Stage 3.

Opening allowance that ties out

The opening allowance defaults by roll-forward from the prior posted run's closing allowance, and a warning fires when it disagrees with the loss-allowance ledger balance carried into the period, so the roll-forward is confirmed before you post.

Movement math

Posting books only the closing-less-opening movement. A falling allowance debits the contra account and credits impairment loss; a nil movement records an immutable zero-movement snapshot without inventing an empty journal entry.

Ageing accuracy

Populate reconstructs every posted receivable's residual as at the reporting date, including later-settled items, validates complete non-overlapping ageing coverage, excludes the allowance account and bulk-writes the measured totals under a run-only lock.

Roll-forward integrity

The opening allowance is defaulted from the prior posted run, and the ledger tie-out negates the contra account's credit balance and excludes this run's own move, so it compares like for like and flags a real mismatch.

Frozen after post

Once posted or reversed, the reporting date, accounts, journal, opening allowance and every bucket are frozen against direct ORM writes, and buckets cannot be added, edited, moved in or deleted, so the recognised figure can never silently drift from the entry that was booked.

One run per date

A partial unique index permits one live run per company and reporting date while retaining cancelled and reversed history, so corrections remain auditable and the active roll-forward stays single-valued.

Workflow reference

Understand the records and controls.

Expected credit loss run

The period-end measurement for one company: reporting date, measurement approach, opening and closing allowance, movement, the three GL accounts and journal, audit stamps for who computed and posted and when, and links to the posted and reversal moves.

ECL provision-matrix bucket

One ageing band in the provision matrix: days-from and days-to bounds, loss rate, IFRS 9 stage, gross carrying amount, and the general-model inputs EAD, LGD, 12-month and lifetime PD, SICR and credit-impaired flags.

Populate and stage helpers

Populate from Receivables action reconstructs reporting-date residuals into validated buckets, while Stage from Ageing uses the run's configured backstops and Stage from Risk applies general-model SICR and credit-impaired flags.

Present-value discounting

A shared present-value factor from the effective interest rate and periods to recovery discounts both the simplified and general ECL (IFRS 9.5.5.17); leave the rate at zero for an undiscounted matrix.

Posting and reversal

journal-entry preparation creates the balanced, sealed movement entry and posts it; reversal action requires and preflights an explicit open date before posting the exact reversal, both restricted to the EH Accounting Manager group with guarded state transitions.

Security and isolation

User preparation navigation, manager posting/reversal/write-off controls, read-only auditor access, global legal-entity isolation and server-side reference validation keep every run and source in its exact company.

Days from

Inclusive lower bound of days past due. Not-yet-due is 0. The stage engine reads this band bound as the bucket's days-past-due signal for the 30/90 day backstops.

Segment

Portfolio segment this band belongs to. Populate from receivables splits the matrix per segment when segments exist; a band without a segment holds the unsegmented rest.

Origin

Populated buckets are created by populate-from-receivables and replaced on each repopulation; manual buckets are the hand-defined matrix and are never restructured by it. Provider buckets hold exposures ingested from an external loss-allowance provider (for example presented-cheque suspense holdings) that have left the open-receivables population.

Effective Interest Rate (%)

Effective interest rate determined at initial recognition, as a percentage. IFRS 9.5.5.17(b): expected credit losses are discounted to the reporting date at the EIR. Leave 0 for an undiscounted provision matrix.

Periods to recovery

Manual number of periods until the expected shortfall is realised. Used only when no maturity date is set; a maturity date derives the period from the reporting date instead.

Maturity date

Expected recovery / maturity date of the exposures in this bucket. When set, the discount period is derived as (maturity date - reporting date) in years on a 365-day basis, so the shortfall is discounted at the EIR over the actual remaining term (IFRS 9.5.5.17(b)).

Stage

IFRS 9 stage. Under the general approach the run's stage engine assigns it each compute from the DPD backstops, qualitative flags and cure probation; the simplified approach always measures lifetime ECL and uses the stage for disclosure only.

Exposure at default

Exposure at default (EAD) for the general 3-stage model: the expected gross carrying amount at the point of default. Populate from receivables fills it with the open residual under the general approach.

SICR

Qualitative override: significant increase in credit risk since initial recognition. The stage engine moves the exposure to Stage 2 (measure lifetime ECL).

Low credit risk

Low-credit-risk exemption (IFRS 9.5.5.10): the entity may assume no significant increase in credit risk, so the stage engine keeps the exposure in Stage 1 despite the 30+ DPD backstop. Does not override credit-impairment or the 90+ DPD backstop.

POCI

Purchased or originated credit-impaired (IFRS 9.5.5.13): always measured at lifetime ECL from initial recognition, never reverts to 12-month ECL, excluded from the stage engine, and disclosed on its own reconciliation line.

Cure streak

Consecutive runs (including this one) the exposure has sat below its staged risk level. It reverts (cures) only once the streak reaches the run's probation length.

Stage auto

Stage derived from the general-model risk flags: Stage 3 if credit-impaired, else Stage 2 if a significant increase in credit risk, else Stage 1.

Ecl

Expected credit loss: gross carrying amount at the loss rate, discounted to present value at the EIR over the effective periods (IFRS 9.5.5.17).

Ecl general

General 3-stage expected credit loss: EAD x LGD x PD, where PD is the 12-month PD for Stage 1 and the lifetime PD for Stage 2, Stage 3 and POCI, probability-weighted over the run's forward-looking scenarios when any exist, and discounted at the EIR.

Ecl effective

The ECL that flows into the run's closing allowance: the general-model figure when the run uses the general approach, otherwise the simplified provision-matrix figure.

Measurement approach

A run measures with exactly one approach. Simplified measures lifetime ECL from the provision matrix (IFRS 9.5.5.15). General measures ECL per bucket from EAD, LGD and the stage's probability of default, with the stage engine re-staging every compute (IFRS 9.5.5.1-5.5.11).

Stage 2 Backstop (DPD)

Days past due beyond which a significant increase in credit risk is presumed and the exposure moves to Stage 2 (rebuttable presumption, IFRS 9.5.5.11).

Cure Probation (runs)

Consecutive runs an exposure must sit below its staged risk level before the stage engine reverts (cures) it, so a one-period improvement does not flip lifetime ECL back to 12-month.

Stage3 pd policy

Controls the general-model PD for non-POCI Stage 3 exposures. Use keyed lifetime PD when credit impairment does not mean a contractual default under the entity's policy. Choose 100% PD when Stage 3 is the entity's default population; LGD and forward-looking recovery scenarios still determine the loss.

Opening allowance

Loss allowance carried at the start of the period; the run posts only the movement to the closing allowance. Defaults by roll-forward from the prior posted run's closing allowance; manual override is allowed and flagged if it disagrees with the ledger.

Reversal date

Explicit accounting date for reversing a posted ECL movement. It must be on or after the original movement and in an open accounting period.

Zero movement posted

Server-owned posting evidence for a run whose rounded closing allowance exactly equals its opening allowance. No artificial zero-value journal is created; this marker lets the immutable period snapshot participate in the ECL roll-forward chain.

Has exposure providers

True when at least one loss-allowance exposure provider is installed (for example presented-cheque suspense holdings), so the populate-including-exposures path has something extra to ingest beyond the open receivables.

Bucket name

Snapshot of the bucket label at transfer time, kept even if the bucket is later removed from a draft run.

Reason

30+ DPD: rebuttable SICR presumption (IFRS 9.5.5.11). 90+ DPD: default backstop (IFRS 9.B5.5.37). Qualitative SICR flag: manual significant-increase-in-credit-risk move to Stage 2. Credit-impaired override: manual impairment move to Stage 3 (IFRS 9.B5.5.37). Cure: reverted after the probation period. POCI: excluded from staging, always lifetime ECL.

Reconcile an allowance movement

Illustrative implementation exercise. The period begins with an allowance recognised in the previous close. Assemble the current exposure and supported impairment inputs, then compare the computed closing requirement with that opening position. The review question is the required change and its evidence, not whether posting the entire closing allowance again produces a balanced journal. Inspect the chosen simplified or general approach, staging and any scenario assumptions used by the run. Resolve an opening mismatch through the supported evidence path and review the final movement. Where a disclosure module consumes posted ECL information, compare its note with the accepted source run instead of manually reproducing the numbers without provenance.

Scope and requirements

Know exactly what you are installing.

It covers trade receivables from the accounting ledger; it does not model loans, debt securities, lease receivables or off-balance-sheet loan commitments and financial guarantee contracts.

It does not calibrate loss rates, probabilities of default or LGD for you; you supply the matrix rates and the general-model risk parameters.

Forward-looking scenarios probability-weight PD and LGD, but the entity remains responsible for supportable scenario design, factors and weights.

Portfolio segments can group commercial customers by country or tags; rules must be mutually exclusive and the entity supplies the risk-calibrated loss profile.

Specific receivable write-offs consume the posted allowance and reconcile the source line; recovery and correction policies still require the entity's own approval evidence.

It produces the run, the matrix and the ledger movement; it does not render a standalone IFRS 7 credit-risk disclosure note or an ECL roll-forward report document.

Standards references describe the accounting subject addressed by the module. They do not establish certification, statutory filing acceptance or suitability for every jurisdiction. Your finance team owns its assumptions, accounting policies and review.

This module computes deferred tax under the IAS 12 balance-sheet liability method. Each temporary difference is entered as a carrying amount and a tax base; the gap, classified taxable or deductible from the item's nature (asset, liability, or tax loss), is measured at the enacted rate resolved from a per-jurisdiction rate table at the reporting date (IAS 12.47), with a reasoned manual override and a statutory fallback. A run compares the closing position to the opening position, splits the movement into rate-change remeasurement versus origination (IAS 12.60(b)), and posts one balanced journal for the movement, recognising it in profit or loss except for lines flagged as OCI-related, whose movement routes to the OCI reserve (IAS 12.61A). Deferred tax assets are capped by line-level recoverable-profit ceilings, carryforward expiry dates, and a run-level projected-profit ceiling, with the unrecognised portion disclosed (IAS 12.81(e)). The run presents gross or net-by-jurisdiction positions (IAS 12.74) and builds the effective-tax-rate reconciliation as auto plus manual reconciling rows that always tie to the total tax expense (IAS 12.81(c)). Posting and reversal are gated to the EH Accounting Manager group and stamped for audit.

IAS 19 Benefits

An IAS 19 accounting layer for defined benefit valuations and defined contribution accruals. Actuarial results are imported inputs; the module has no actuarial engine. It computes the ledger mechanics for net interest, service cost, settlements, non-recycling OCI remeasurements, the asset ceiling, tied obligation and plan-asset rollforwards, and one sealed entry per valuation. A rendered IAS 19.140-141 disclosure schedule presents the recognised liability or asset net per plan. Short-term benefits, other long-term benefits, termination benefits, and reimbursement rights are explicitly outside this module's scope.

IFRS 13 Fair Value

This module records and posts fair value under IFRS 13 with IFRS 9 classification. Each item (Fair value item (IFRS 13)) is classified in the three-level hierarchy by input observability, its valuation technique is captured as market, income or cost approach, and for Level 3 the significant unobservable inputs are documented. An IFRS 9.4.1 engine derives the classification from a structured SPPI questionnaire (fixed dates, solely principal and interest, no leverage, no contingent returns) and the business model (hold to collect, hold to collect and sell, other): amortised cost, FVOCI debt, FVOCI equity election or FVTPL, with the P&L/OCI routing derived from the classification rather than chosen by hand, and nonsense combinations blocked (a liability cannot take the equity election, a derivative is always FVTPL, the election is irrevocable once posted). Remeasuring posts the change from the current carrying amount to fair value in one balanced entry and rolls the carrying amount forward. Derecognising posts the disposal entry and, atomically, settles the accumulated OCI reserve: FVOCI debt recycles it to profit or loss, an FVOCI equity election transfers it within equity to retained earnings and never through profit or loss. The Level 3 roll-forward reconciliation (IFRS 13.93(e)) can be ledger-fed from the posted entries and cannot be closed unless its closing balance ties to the fair value, and a per-item sensitivity table (input shock times elasticity) produces the IFRS 13.93(h) quantitative disclosure.

IAS 37 Provisions

A dedicated IAS 37 provision register for Odoo 20 Community. Each provision is recognised at the present value of the undiscounted best estimate, posting a debit to the expense account and a credit to the provision liability. Where the time value of money is material, a pre-tax discount rate and a settlement horizon discount the estimate, and the Unwind Discount action accretes the carrying amount towards the undiscounted figure by posting the period finance cost. Remeasure books a change in estimate through profit or loss, Utilise settles expenditure against the liability, and Reverse writes back a provision no longer required. Contingent liabilities and contingent assets are held for disclosure only and any attempt to post them is refused. Posting is gated to the EH Accounting Manager group and every generated journal entry is sealed against edit.

IFRS 2 Share-based Pay

A dedicated IFRS 2 share-based payment engine for Odoo 20 Community. Plans define the settlement mode, vesting terms and condition kind; grants under a plan carry the instruments, the grant-date fair value and an updatable forfeiture estimate. Period runs compute the cumulative expense per IFRS 2 and post the period delta as a sealed journal entry: equity-settled awards accrue grant-date fair value times the instruments expected to vest times the vested fraction (each graded tranche over its own vesting period), estimates true up through the current period, non-market failures reverse in full while market-condition failures never reverse once service is rendered, and cash-settled awards remeasure the liability to current fair value each period until settlement. Beneficial modifications spread their incremental fair value over the remaining vesting period, cancellation accelerates the unrecognised balance immediately, and a built-in valuation helper prices options with Black-Scholes or a Cox-Ross-Rubinstein binomial tree. The plan carries an IFRS 2.45 rollforward of granted, forfeited, exercised and expired instruments with the weighted average exercise price. Posting is gated to the EH Accounting Manager group and every generated journal entry is sealed against edit.
